Common questions

How do I earn Kibble? Complete levels, claim the daily reward, or buy a coin pack in the Shop. Everything can be earned by playing.

Do purchases transfer between devices? Coin packs are tied to your Apple ID. Progress is stored on your device.

How do I reset my progress? Settings → Reset Progress.
