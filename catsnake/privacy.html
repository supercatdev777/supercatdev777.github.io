Privacy Policy

CatSnake — Effective 5 September 2026

The short version: CatSnake collects no personal data, has no accounts, shows no ads, and makes no network requests. Everything stays on your device.

What data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information, and it requires no sign-in.

What stays on your device

Your progress — level stars, coins ("Kibble"), owned cosmetics, and settings — is stored only on your device. It never leaves your device and we cannot access it. Deleting the app removes this data (a small value may persist in the device Keychain by design so a best score can survive reinstall).

What we do not do

  • We do not run any servers or make network requests.
  • We do not require an account.
  • We do not show ads, use analytics SDKs, or track you across apps.
  • We do not sell or share your information.

Purchases

Optional "Kibble" coin packs are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles these transactions under Apple's privacy policy; we never receive your payment details or any identifying information.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages, collects no data, and shows no ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
